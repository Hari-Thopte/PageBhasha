A FEW LITTLE DOUBTS, SOLVED

Good questions.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Open Learning Mode and choose See Demo for a guided example. Live lessons use the learning service connected by the site administrator.

Is it translating my page word for word?

The live tutor is instructed to explain concepts at your class level and connect them to source excerpts. You can keep English subject terms alongside your preferred language.

What happens to my textbook page?

Your page is sent to the connected learning service when you generate a lesson. Uploaded pages are not saved. Your latest notes and preferences stay in this browser until you clear them.

Can I rely on every answer?

AI can make mistakes. Source excerpts help you compare the explanation with your page, but they are not a guarantee of correctness. Ask your teacher about important or confusing details.
